# DISCOVERLY — Google Play Store listing draft

## App name
DISCOVERLY

## Short description
Discover businesses, post needs and connect with service providers across India.

## Full description
DISCOVERLY helps individuals and organizations discover businesses and services, share what they need, and connect with relevant providers.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Discover businesses by name, industry and city.
• Create a business profile and introduce your services.
• Post a requirement and reach relevant businesses.
• Receive and review business quotations.
• Manage incoming enquiries and business opportunities.
• Stay informed with in-app notifications.

BUILT FOR BOTH SIDES
Whether you are looking for a service or offering one, DISCOVERLY brings business discovery and customer requirements into one place.

A NETWORK THAT IS GROWING
DISCOVERLY is an early-stage marketplace. Business availability varies by location. Listings are shown after the required owner confirmation and platform review. Some locations and categories may have limited results while the network grows.

SAFETY NOTE
Always verify a business independently before entering into an agreement, making a payment or sharing sensitive information. DISCOVERLY provides discovery and enquiry tools; it does not guarantee a transaction or independently guarantee every claim made by a listing.
